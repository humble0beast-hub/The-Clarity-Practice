THE CLARITY PRACTICE — PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and content
- styles.css: responsive visual design
- script.js: mobile navigation and demo form behavior

PREVIEW:
Open index.html in a browser. An internet connection is needed for Google Fonts and the Unsplash photography used in this demo.

BEFORE USING FOR A REAL CLIENT:
- Replace the demo photography with the client's approved images.
- Connect the contact form to a real form handler or booking service.
- Replace fictional testimonials and any sample business details with approved, genuine content.
- Add the client's domain, privacy policy and legal information as needed.

The layout is responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
